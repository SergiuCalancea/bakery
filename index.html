Our story

A small bakery with a big oven

We started in 2009 with a single oven and a sourdough starter gifted by a baker from Bukovina. We still feed that same starter today, every single day.

No improvers, no preservatives, no premixes. Just flour, water, salt and time. Lots of time.

How we bake

From starter to your table

  1. 01

    Feed the starter

    In the evening, the starter gets fresh flour and water and wakes up overnight.

  2. 02

    Knead

    The dough is worked slowly, with rests, so the gluten develops naturally.

  3. 03

    Slow rise

    18 to 24 hours in the cold. That's where the flavor and easy digestion come from.

  4. 04

    Hearth-bake

    At 250°C, with steam, on stone. The result: a crust that sings.
